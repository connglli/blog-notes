# Influential paper summaries

This directory covers retrospective paper-impact awards linked from the SIGPLAN and SIGOPS award pages. It excludes annual best-paper awards, dissertations, and awards to people or software systems. Each work follows the repository's [summary template](../TEMPLATE.md); its source field distinguishes publication year from award year. A paper recognized by more than one award has one summary with both credits.

The official recipient lists and supplementary award announcements define the selection:

* POPL Most Influential Paper: https://www.sigplan.org/Awards/POPL/
* PLDI Most Influential Paper: https://www.sigplan.org/Awards/PLDI/
* ICFP Most Influential Paper: https://www.sigplan.org/Awards/ICFP/ and the conference history, which includes the 2026 recipient: https://icfpconference.org/history.html
* OOPSLA Most Influential Paper, including the retrospective 1986-1996 selections: https://www.sigplan.org/Awards/OOPSLA/
* Joint SIGARCH/SIGPLAN/SIGOPS ASPLOS Influential Paper: https://www.sigarch.org/benefit/awards/acm-sigarch-sigplan-sigops-asplos-influential-paper-award/
* SIGOPS Hall of Fame: https://www.sigops.org/awards/hof/; supplementary 2026 announcements: https://anakli.inf.ethz.ch/ and https://drkp.net/
* Edsger W. Dijkstra Prize, including its PODC Influential-Paper Award predecessor: https://www.podc.org/dijkstra/
* EuroSys Test-of-Time: https://www.eurosys.org/awards/test-of-time-award

Filenames use an award-series prefix, a publication year, and a short work name. Technical claims are checked against papers or author-written technical sources, not inferred from award citations alone. Some older works require author dissertations, technical reports, documentation, retrospectives, or cached paper passages when the complete original is inaccessible. The staged-only format hook covers this directory; structural checks do not establish technical correctness.
